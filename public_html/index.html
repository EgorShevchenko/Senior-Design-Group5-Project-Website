U.K.V.S

Universal Knight Vision Scope

Senior Design Fall 2026 - Spring 2027 | Group 5

Project Overview

The Universal Knight Vision Scope (U.K.V.S.) is an magnified optical device designed to enhance visibility in low-light conditions. It utilizes advanced imaging technology to provide clear and detailed visuals, making it ideal for various applications such as security, surveillance, and outdoor activities. The U.K.V.S. is compact, lightweight, and user-friendly, ensuring that users can easily operate it in different environments. The device is equipped with features such as adjustable brightness and a durable design to withstand harsh conditions.
